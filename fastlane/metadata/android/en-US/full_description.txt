BatteryExpert manages rechargeable battery data and directly controls the SkyRC MC5000 (4-slot charger) over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

Features:
* Cell-type database with datasheet data per cell type (manufacturer, model, chemistry, capacity, charge/discharge parameters, NiMH delta-peak, ...)
* Cell inventory: assign individual cells to a type (origin, status, location)
* Live monitoring: voltage, current, capacity, time and internal resistance of the four slots in real time, including a voltage/current chart
* Cell assessment: internal-resistance and capacity/SOH tests with a traffic-light recommendation (ok / watch / sort out)
* Control: send charge/discharge parameters directly to the MC5000
* Optional AI research: look up cell-type data via Gemini or DeepSeek (requires your own API key; the app works fully without it)
* Import/Export: back up and exchange data as JSON (CSV for test results)
